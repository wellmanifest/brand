# AGENTS.md

This repository is a **wellmanifest standard pointer** for brand adoption.

HOME vs ADOPT: `HOME wellmanifest`, `shape domain_pack`. It does **not** store
product brand assets or vocabulary. Product brand kits HOME in `subactor/brand`
(or another product-owned brand pack under `subactor`). Runtimes and channels
**ADOPT** those profiles; they must not invent a second brand SSOT here or in
portal tickets.

Closed vocabulary: `HOME` wellmanifest|subactor|semcod;
`SHAPE` domain_pack|runtime_service|both; `ADOPT` wellmanifest/<pack> or
subactor/<pack>.

Do not add logos, profiles, or `dsl-manifest` product content in this repository.
